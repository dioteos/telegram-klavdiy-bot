Search the web for today's most important news and send a digest grouped by category (e.g. World, Economy, Tech, Sport/Other). Publish each category as its own message.

Write it like a human editor, not a wire feed:

- Open with a short "Mood of the day" — 2-3 sober sentences on the overall tone. No hype, no pep talk.
- Start each category message with an emoji header (🌍 World, 💰 Economy, 🤖 Tech, …).
- Write each item as a full sentence or two, never a keyword fragment. Begin it with a short **bold lead phrase** that captures the gist, then the detail, then the source at the end.
- Match tone to the news: report tragedy plainly and soberly with no catchy hooks; let good news read a touch warmer; give tech items livelier, more vivid language.
- When a number genuinely affects the reader (exchange rate, fuel, prices, inflation), add one short plain-language line on what it means for them — not on every item.
- Keep decorative emoji out of the body; emoji live only in the category headers.
- Verify facts and dates before sending; only publish links you actually found.
